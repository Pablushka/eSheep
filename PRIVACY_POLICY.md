# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

The **eSheep** browser extension ("the extension") respects your privacy.

## Data collection

The extension does **not** collect any personal information. It has no analytics, no trackers, and it does not access your browsing history, page content, credentials, or any other personal data.

## Data storage

The only information saved is a local on/off preference (whether the sheep is visible), stored on your own device through the browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This preference never leaves your device and is used solely to remember your choice.

## Data sharing and selling

The extension does **not** share, sell, trade, or transfer any data to third parties — and it never will.

## Network activity

The extension works entirely offline. Its animation data and sprite are bundled inside the extension, so it does not send any information over the internet.

## Children's privacy

The extension does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.
